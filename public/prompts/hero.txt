Abstract vector illustration for a blood donation brand called HAEMA.
Transparent background. Square 1:1 composition.

Central motif: a single elegant blood drop, smooth and rounded, filled 
with a solid deep red (#be151b). Surrounding the drop are thin, flowing 
line-art ribbons that curve outward like ripples or lifelines that is suggesting 
circulation, connection, and the ripple effect of one donation saving 
multiple lives. The ribbons are drawn in charcoal (#323232) with white 
(#ffffff) highlights where they overlap the drop.

Optionally, two tiny minimal icons floating near the drop that is a heart and 
a circle of connected dots that is rendered in thin strokes, no fills, 
evenly spaced.

Style: pure flat SVG vector illustration. Crisp geometric shapes, 
uniform stroke width (4 units on a 512×512 canvas), rounded line caps 
and joins. No gradients, no textures, no shadows, no 3D. 
Feels like a modern icon set or geometric logo mark scaled up.

Palette:
- primary:    #be151b  (deep red that is the drop)
- secondary:  #ffffff  (white that is highlights, negative space)
- tertiary:   #323232  (charcoal that is thin line work only)
- transparent background

Mood: calm, premium, minimal, hopeful. Medical but warm. 
No gore, no needles, no hospital imagery.

Composition: centered, symmetrical, generous negative space around 
the edges so it sits comfortably inside a circle or square container.

--ar 1:1 --style raw --v 6 --no background, gradients, shadows, 3D, 
text, watermark, gore, needles --stylize 50
